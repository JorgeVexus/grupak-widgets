Responsible management and environmental performance

Our Corrugado Hidalgo plant holds the international EDGE Advanced (Excellence in Design for Greater Efficiencies) certification, which recognizes sustainable buildings for their resource efficiency in water, energy, and materials.

This milestone reinforces Grupak's commitment to sustainable construction and environmental innovation, positioning the plant as the first in the Latin American paper industry to achieve this certification, consolidating our leadership in developing responsible and resilient infrastructure.

We are the first company in Latin America to have Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs) verified under the International EPD System. These third-party verified declarations provide clear, comparable environmental data across our products' life cycles: paper, corrugated sheets, and conventional boxes, supporting our clients in their sustainability strategies and informed purchasing decisions.

Through this, we foster a value chain grounded in environmental traceability and transparency.

Environmental product declarations

Responsible
water stewardship

We manage water responsibly to promote efficient usage, mitigate environmental impact, and drive water reuse across our manufacturing operations.

At the close of last year, we reused 206,438.74 m³ of water across manufacturing processes and auxiliary services within our plants.

We maintain an average technical capacity to reuse up to 29% of treated effluent water.

Additionally, because our facilities operate in regions with high water stress, we treat 100% of the water we use, ensuring rigorous management and regulatory compliance.

Clean
energy consumption

We drive energy efficiency and the adoption of clean energy sources across our operations. In 2025, 74% of our total energy consumption came from clean sources. Through our cogeneration facility, Grupak Energía, we generate energy harnessing methane and associated gases.

That same year, our solar infrastructure expansion marked a major milestone: solar energy accounted for 41% of total consumption at the Toluca Supply Center and 21% at the Puebla Supply Center, reinforcing our renewable energy portfolio.

Material
circularity

64.41% of waste generated across our manufacturing processes is recovered or reintroduced into recycling streams. Manufacturing scrap is systematically gathered and fed back into the fiber recovery system, guaranteeing its reuse as raw material.

When certain residues cannot be directly reincorporated due to physical or chemical specifications, they are channeled to certified partners who transform them into alternative products, such as roofing sheets made from recycled fiber, preventing landfill disposal.

Additionally, we promote electronic waste recycling through dedicated collection initiatives. Thanks to employee participation, last year we collected 655 electronic devices, avoiding approximately 5 metric tons of CO2e—an emissions reduction equivalent to driving an average car roughly 20,000 kilometers.

Impact reduction

Our FSC® Chain of Custody certification guarantees the responsible sourcing of all fibers and materials we process.

By using 100% recycled fiber, we prevent the felling of 4.6 million trees, actively contributing to forest conservation that shelters over 80% of global terrestrial biodiversity.

We continue identifying opportunities and initiatives that strengthen biodiversity protection and systematically decrease our environmental footprints.

Furthermore, we are actively establishing clear targets that consolidate our corporate climate strategy, driving long-term emissions reductions across our manufacturing footprint.
