Sustainability Strategy

Our sustainability strategy is built around three core pillars that enable us to advance with environmental and social responsibility across our operations and alongside all key stakeholders.

The formulation of this strategy, along with the governance of priority topics, is established by Senior Management and approved by the Chairman of the Board of Directors.

It is developed taking into account the company's overarching business strategy and the insights from our double materiality assessment. Ongoing oversight is conducted by the Sustainability Committee and specialized Working Commissions, comprised of employee representatives from diverse operational areas, structured into:

Commission Business
Ethics
Commission Environment
Commission Community
Engagement
Commission Clients &
Suppliers
