Our history

Beginning of operations

Our history began in 1957 in San Cristóbal Ecatepec. Recognizing a clear market opportunity for corrugated transport packaging, especially in the food industry, visionary investors joined forces to establish Grupak.

Opening of Cuernavaca plant | Commissioning of Unipak — our first manufacturing hub.

In 1962, with the commissioning of Unipak (Cuernavaca), we established our first manufacturing hub. We quickly understood that continuity and quality are best guaranteed by mastering the supply chain.

Entry into paper manufacturing | Vertical integration with liner and medium production (from fiber to box).

In 1966, we executed backward integration and began producing liner and medium paper, laying the foundation for our signature vertical integration: "from fiber to box."

Acquisition of Sta. Clara supply center to
strengthen our supply model

During the 1980s, we accelerated corporate professionalization: in 1983, we completed a strategic acquisition focused on securing recovered fiber — the foundation of our products — significantly reinforcing our supply chain model.

In 2011, we integrated the Cuautitlán center, broadening our raw material recovery reach.

Puebla (2013)

We extended our logistics reach and supply network with the Puebla branch in 2013.

and Querétaro (2014).

We established the Querétaro Recovery Center to lead recycled fiber collection across the Bajío region.

Opening of Hidalgo plant, World-class paper mill consolidates leadership in 100% recycled paper.

A major technological leap came with our Hidalgo Paper Mill (2015), where we invested in a world-class paper mill that consolidated our nationwide leadership in 100% recycled paper.

Launch of WOWPAK, Start of digital packaging operations (high graphics and point-of-sale differentiation).

Subsequently, we inaugurated our Digital Packaging Plant in 2021, taking a leap beyond protecting products to driving differentiation in retail through high-definition digital printing.

Acquisition of cogeneration power plant in Hidalgo.

In 2023, the acquisition of an on-site energy cogeneration plant and the launch of Corrugado Hidalgo enhanced energy efficiency, capacity, and resilience, empowering growth with lower total cost of ownership and dependable turnaround times for our clients.

Beginning of operations at Corrugado Hidalgo.

Our mission transcends conventional manufacturing of high-quality paper, boxes, sheets, and packaging. We embed innovation, sustainability, and environmental stewardship as cornerstone pillars of our daily operations. Through a comprehensive circular economy model, we minimize resource consumption and solid waste, manufacturing our packaging from 100% post-consumer recycled fiber. This commitment solidifies our leadership in the packaging industry with efficient, superior-quality processes.
