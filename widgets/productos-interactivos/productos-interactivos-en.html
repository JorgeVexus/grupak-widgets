Paper

Grupak offers a comprehensive portfolio of linerboard and medium specifically formulated for corrugated containerboard manufacturing and packaging conversion.

Each paper grade addresses distinct performance demands for strength, runnability, and print quality, ranging from standard retail packaging to heavy-duty industrial and agricultural applications.

We place our clients at the core of our operations: providing weekly delivery confirmations and specialized technical consulting to enhance and optimize packaging performance and competitiveness.

Our containerboard serves diverse industrial applications as the prime raw material for corrugated board production, multiwall bags, food packaging, edge protectors, paper cores, and specialized product cushioning.

ClassicPak N

Standard containerboard line

Multipurpose containerboard meeting nationwide market specifications, with basis weights from 120 gsm to 280 gsm.

Primary applications:

Paper bags, lightweight packaging, and general-purpose retail corrugated boxes.

UltraPak H

High-performance containerboard line

Basis weights from 120 gsm to 200 gsm.

Primary applications:

Demanding applications requiring maximum tensile strength at lower basis weights, rivaling imported virgin-fiber performance.

FortePak R

Heavy-duty industrial containerboard

Basis weights from 130 gsm to 270 gsm.

Primary applications:

Heavy-duty boxes requiring high stacking strength for major appliances, automotive parts, and industrial goods.

AgricoPak A

Agricultural containerboard line

Basis weights from 120 gsm to 260 gsm.

Primary applications:

Produce boxes transporting fresh fruits, vegetables, and moisture-exposed goods through cold-chain logistics.

Energy

We are committed to efficient electricity generation and environmental stewardship. Our on-site cogeneration plant enables the simultaneous production of clean electrical power and thermal energy.

Superior Energy Efficiency

While conventional thermal power plants utilize only 34–40% of fuel energy, Grupak's cogeneration technology reaches up to 80% overall energy efficiency.

Reduced Environmental Impact

By maximizing fuel efficiency, we cut greenhouse emissions and lower our corporate carbon footprint.

Reliable Supply & Continuity

We generate a substantial portion of our operational power, ensuring uninterrupted manufacturing continuity for recycled paper and packaging production.
