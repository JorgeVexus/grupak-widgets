Environment

Product Life Cycle

At Grupak, we conduct our operations with a focus on responsible production, promoting the efficient use of natural resources and reducing environmental impacts across our processes, embedding circular economy principles. The environmental management of our operations is backed by ISO 14001:2015 certification and FSC® Chain of Custody certification, guaranteeing the responsible origin of the materials we use.

Our model prioritizes the use of recycled fiber, responsible stewardship of water and waste, as well as energy efficiency in paper and packaging production. Through this closed-loop approach, we recover post-consumer paper and cardboard, convert it into new packaging, and reintegrate it into the productive cycle.

In 2025, we recovered 273,007 tons of cardboard as raw material. This enabled us to use 100% recycled paper across our core operations, preventing the harvesting of 4.6 million trees and saving 682,000 m³ of landfill space — equivalent to 272 Olympic swimming pools.

01

Fiber Recovery

We directly collect used paper and cardboard that can still be recycled. This raw material is sourced from evaluated suppliers to ensure optimal quality.

02

Fiber Sorting & Ingestion

We receive the recovered fiber, classify it, and inspect it to ensure it meets the strict requirements needed for paper remanufacturing.

03

Paper Production

The fiber is cleaned, pulped, and transformed into new paper. It is then dried, wound, and prepared into high-performance paper reels ready for converting.

04

Corrugated Converting

Paper reels are combined to form corrugated cardboard. Structural properties are engineered, and sheets or custom boxes are manufactured according to exact customer specifications.

05

Distribution & Logistics

Finished paper reels, corrugated sheets, or converted boxes are warehouse-managed, quality-checked, and safely dispatched to clients on schedule.

06

Use & End of Life

Customers use our packaging to protect and deliver their products. Afterwards, the packaging material is recovered to restart the circular cycle.
