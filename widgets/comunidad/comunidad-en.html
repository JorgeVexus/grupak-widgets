Community

Contributing to human well-being

At Grupak, we understand that our development is closely linked to the progress of the communities where we operate. Therefore, we drive actions that promote well-being and strengthen shared social responsibility.

In 2025, our initiatives benefited more than 31,000 people through local impact programs, cross-sector alliances, and the active engagement of our employees.

Social Support and Relief

We collaborate with local authorities and organizations to address priority community needs across our operating regions.

Through concrete actions, we provide relief during emergency situations by donating boxes for food supplies, delivering medical furniture in good working condition, and supporting traditional cultural celebrations, alongside other community assistance programs.

In 2025, these initiatives benefited more than 30,000 people across Hidalgo, Cuernavaca, and Toluca, strengthening community response capacity and helping improve welfare and living conditions across neighboring areas.

Community Development

Through our Reciclón program, we promote environmental education and waste separation culture across schools neighboring our manufacturing facilities. Through training sessions and cardboard collection drives, we encourage school communities to engage in responsible waste valorization.

Over the past three years, this initiative has benefited more than 7,000 people across 20 schools. In recognition of their dedication, we donate supplies and equipment that enhance educational environments, reinforcing our shared commitment to environmental protection.

Additionally, we contribute to the revitalization of public spaces through targeted initiatives aimed at improving neighborhood environments and quality of life for our communities.

Fostering Education

In 2025, we launched the second cohort of the Gradúa-T Program, an initiative providing full university scholarships to eight vulnerable young individuals to study at leading private universities.

Through this program, we reaffirm our conviction that education is a powerful catalyst for building a fairer, more sustainable future for students, their families, and their communities.

Throughout their academic path, scholars participate in direct mentorship sessions with our executive leaders, who guide them from their first semester to build professional skills and introduce them to business leadership.

We also sponsored the 2025 edition of the National Congress of Renewable Energy Students, where we shared insights with more than 200 youths on the vital role of energy efficiency in accelerating a circular economy. As part of this effort, we awarded scholarships to 37 students from various regions across the country to join this academic forum.
